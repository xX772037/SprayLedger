For drone and ground sprayer applicators in South Dakota

Your spray records, done before you leave the field.

Load the drone's flight log or track the sprayer on your phone. SprayLedger maps what got covered, checks the weather, and fills in the state application record and a report for your customer.

  • No account or sign-up
  • Records stay on your device
  • Big buttons, big text

Three steps, one job

  1. 1

    Pick the field

    Type the address or draw the corners on the map. Land owners and the neighbors downwind show up for eight southeast South Dakota counties.

  2. 2

    Spray it

    Load the flight log when you land, or track a tractor sprayer live. Green shows what got covered, red shows anything missed.

  3. 3

    Hand it over

    Download the customer report and the South Dakota application record (ARSD 12:56:07:01) as PDFs, ready to text or email.

What it does for you

  • Coverage map

    Missed spots are numbered, with a GPS point you can drive straight back to.

  • Weather check

    Wind, gusts, Delta T and inversion risk, hour by hour, with the best times to spray.

  • Tank and time plan

    Gallons to mix, tank loads and how long the field will take.

  • Your equipment, saved

    Enter a drone or a tractor sprayer once and pick it on every job after.

  • Product list

    Common herbicides, fungicides and insecticides with EPA numbers and re-entry times.

  • Nothing forgotten

    A checklist shows anything the state record still needs before you file it.
